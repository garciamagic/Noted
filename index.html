YOU

There’s a voice I seem to know by heart,
That feels like home when we’re apart.
A certain laugh, a certain smile,
That makes the world stop for a while.

There’s a way you seem to say my name,
That makes the old days feel the same.
You know the things I try to hide,
And see the me I keep inside.

You know the stories I forget,
The ones we haven’t written yet.
You find the words I fail to say,
And somehow hear them anyway.

You make the little moments last,
The future now, the present past.
You find the right side of my wrong,
You make a short goodbye feel long.

You know the sound of being there,
The little ways you show you care.
A look, a joke, a passing glance,
A second thought, a second chance.

You’re inside every photograph,
In every song, in every laugh.
In every day, and every year.
In every smile, and every tear.

You turn my silence into sound,
And make my lost feel like my found.
You make a lifetime feel so fast,
And every moment seem to last.

If forever had another day,
And another day could never fade,
I’d give forever just one name,
I’d call it you, and you’re to blame.

Language

Instructions

Noted looks like a poem in your Notes app. They read it out loud, and the last line ends with the name of the person it reminded them of.

Part 1: Setting Up

  1. Put it on your home screen. Upload the Noted folder to your host (the same way as OneLock), open the link in Safari, then tap Share → Add to Home Screen. It shows up as “Noted”.
  2. Open Settings. Tap the ••• button at the top right, on the Notes list or the poem. Set your phrases and your notes list.
  3. Allow permissions. In Settings → Permissions, tap Allow for Microphone, and for Motion if you use face-down restart. Do this again any time you've fully closed Noted.
  4. Rehearse. Turn on Rehearsal Mode and run the routine out loud a few times. Turn it off when you're done.

Part 2: Performing

The poem stays as written. The name goes into the list hidden below it.

  1. Open the poem. Tap the poem note, then double-tap anywhere on the poem to start listening (the orange dot appears at the top). Hand them the phone: “This is a poem I wrote for a friend. Read it out loud.”
  2. Halfway, stop them. Ask: “Does this remind you of anyone?” Then: “Who?” They say a name, and you say “Interesting.” The mic turns off.
  3. Get their name, any time. In Sentence format, ask “What's your name?” and say “Nice…” after they answer. Before, during, or after the poem, in either order. If the mic is already off, double-tap first.
  4. They finish the poem. It ends with “you”.
  5. Show the list. Scroll down past the poem to the list. The newest entry has the name.

Part 3: Name Mode

Turn on Name Mode in Settings to put the name right into the poem's last line.

  1. Same start. Open the poem, double-tap, hand it over.
  2. Halfway, stop them. Ask who it reminds them of. They say a name and you say “Interesting.” The name goes into the last line, which is still below the screen.
  3. They keep reading. They scroll down, and the last line has the name.
  4. If it misses and they read “you”, the list backup takes over. Turn the phone face down (or let Automatic restart start it), ask again, then show the list.

Tip: In Name Mode, ask before they reach the last stanza. Once the last line has been on screen for about 2 seconds, it stops waiting.
